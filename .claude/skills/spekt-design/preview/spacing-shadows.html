Elevation - 4 levels, all soft

On dark UI, dark drop shadows are nearly invisible, so PO Agent elevation is carried by a light rim: a 1px outer ring plus a 1px inset highlight at the top edge, both rgba-white. The rim grows brighter as elevation rises (4% → 7% → 10% → 13%). A dark drop is still layered underneath, but it’s a secondary cue.
