## Appendix A: Product Requirements

The following keywords are used throughout this appendix and carry specific meaning:

- **SHALL** — A must-have requirement. No exceptions; if this requirement is not met, the product is not ready to ship.
- **SHOULD** — A prefer-to-have requirement. Exceptions must be discussed and agreed upon by the team; otherwise, this requirement is assumed to be in scope.
- **MAY** — A like-to-have requirement. This requirement is NOT considered in scope unless it is explicitly determined to have negligible impact on timeline, budget, and technical complexity. Any implementation must be agreed upon.
- **SHALL NOT / SHOULD NOT / MAY NOT** — Negative forms of the above. The "NOT" carries the same force as the positive keyword.

Requirements are stated from the perspective of the customer or stakeholder and should NOT dictate implementation unless explicitly required by the customer or stakeholder.
